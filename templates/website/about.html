Who We Are

Born from a Passion for Wilderness Flight

{{ site_settings.about_story }}

Our facility was conceived to bring world-standard canopy tour experiences to Indian adventure tourism. Operating on rigid environmental protection protocols, every platform and anchor is designed to harmonize with forest topography without damaging tree root systems or wildlife migration trails.

Aerospace Safety

Dual stainless-steel wire lines with a combined breaking strain of over 28,000 kg. High-grade ZipStop magnetic decelerators ensure smooth, effortless arrivals.

Elite Certified Crew

Our riggers and jumpmasters hold certified mountaineering and aerial rescue credentials, undergoing recertification and medical fitness testing quarterly.

Eco-Conscious Design

Solar-powered dispatch stations, strict zero-plastic valley zones, and continuous participation in regional reforestation programs.

Base Camp & Sanctuary

Our Location: The Western Ghats Gateway

Perched at an elevation of 1,100 meters above sea level, Black Eagles Zipline offers a crisp mountain climate year-round. Overlooking the legendary Eagle Peak valley, riders catch panoramic sunrises and rolling mist blankets during early morning flights.
